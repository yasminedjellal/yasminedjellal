## Yasmine Djellal

Yasmine Djellal is a strategic communications advisor and the Founder & Principal of Third Culture Comms, with almost two decades inside the World Bank Group, international development sector, U.S. Government, and private sector.

Third Culture Comms is a Washington D.C.-area strategic communications consultancy for senior executives, international organizations, and policy voices.

Yasmine Djellal builds strategic communications and narrative strategies for leaders who shape lasting outcomes. Multilateral institutions, governments, foundations, and organizations across the Global South, in a multipolar era where the story an institution tells is part of what it can defend.

Third Culture Comms works across international affairs and international development, with multilateral institutions, foundations, governments, NGOs, think tanks, senior leaders, and mission-driven businesses and individuals operating in complex global environments.

Formation across cultures and languages, and nearly two decades building that fluency inside the world's most consequential development institutions and private sector. Arabic, English, French, and Spanish as a genuine thinking and advising capability, not a line on a CV.

No junior teams. No account managers. Every engagement runs through her directly.

### Practice areas

**Strategic Counsel.** Ongoing embedded advisory for principals and leadership teams.

**Narrative Architecture.** The foundational story work, built to hold under pressure.

**Principal Voice.** Communications for senior leaders and heads of institution.

**Research & Insights.** Public opinion and narrative intelligence.

### Regions

Africa, MENA, and the Gulf. Based in the Washington D.C. area, working globally.

### Writing

The Narrative Power Project, an analytical publication on governance, narrative strategy, and geopolitics. Published in Diplomatika, Jeune Afrique, Project Syndicate, the World Economic Forum, Financial Times, and many other outlets.

### Background

World Bank Group Senior Communications roles including in the Office of the World Bank Senior Vice President and Group General Counsel, the front offices of the World Bank Group Gender Group, the Education Global Practice, Trade and Competitiveness Global Practice, the Governance Global Practice, the World Bank Institute Innovation Labs, and the Global Environment Facility.

Yasmine Djellal was also appointed by the World Bank President David Malpass to lead the communications for the first-ever Anti-Racism Task Force.

Following her World Bank Group career, Yasmine Djellal held roles at Nathan Associates and the Cadmus Group, serving the U.S. Government and Foreign Assistance under USAID.
