## Yasmine Djellal

Yasmine Djellal is a strategic communications advisor and the Founder & Principal of Third Culture Comms, with 18+ years inside the World Bank Group and the international development sector.

Third Culture Comms is a Washington D.C.-area strategic communications consultancy for senior executives, international organizations, and policy voices.

Based in the Washington D.C. area. Works in English, French, Arabic, and Spanish.

### Focus

Narrative strategy, institutional accountability, and communications for multilateral institutions, foundations, governments, and mission-driven organizations, with regional depth across Africa, MENA, and the Gulf.

### Writing

The Narrative Power Project, an analytical publication on governance, narrative strategy, and geopolitics. Also published in Diplomatika, Jeune Afrique, and Lapham's Quarterly.
